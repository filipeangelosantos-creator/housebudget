Tips

  • You'll see a preview before anything is saved — nothing is imported yet.
  • Always check the preview on a PDF. The table is rebuilt from the page layout, so glance at the dates and amounts before importing.
  • Re-uploading overlapping statements is safe: duplicates are skipped automatically.
  • The app remembers each bank's layout after the first confirmed import.
  • A scanned or photographed statement has no text to read — for those, ask your bank for a CSV or Excel export.
